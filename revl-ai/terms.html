7. Caching and masking

Caching and masking are off unless you turn them on for a request. You are responsible for deciding when to use them, for configuring them and for testing them on your own workload.

  • Caching is exact-match. When you turn it on, a later identical request from the same tenant with the same provider key can be answered from the cache until the TTL you set runs out. The TTL is between 60 seconds and 24 hours. You decide whether a cached answer is acceptable for a given request.
  • Masking is pattern based. It will miss things. It is not applied to tool definitions, tool call arguments, or image and document data. In the current version it is not available for streaming requests.
  • Masking is not a compliance control by itself. Do not rely on it as your only safeguard for personal data or secrets.

We do not promise that caching will reduce your provider costs by any amount, or that masking will catch every value.
